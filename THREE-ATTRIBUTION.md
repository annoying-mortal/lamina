# Three-dimensional archive attribution

The three-dimensional archive scene in `src/archive/` and the cassette model in
`public/assets/archive-cassette.glb` are adapted from
[LBEILC/RhineLabUI](https://github.com/LBEILC/RhineLabUI), revision `12cc5e4`.
The original scene, materials, physical extraction, array inertia and shading
remain the basis of this local prototype. The opening sequence, original brand
labels and separate model inspector are not included. Article mapping, lifecycle,
container input handling and personal labels have been adapted for this blog.

The cassette is retained as a prototype asset. Its original design is inspired
by the Rhine Lab promotional film from Arknights. This prototype is not endorsed
by Hypergryph. Any brand-specific final geometry should be replaced with original
artwork before a public branded release.
