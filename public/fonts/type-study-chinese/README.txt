Preview-only Chinese webfont subsets

Study Han Sans: Source Han Sans SC 2.005, Light (300) and Regular (400).
Study WenKai: LXGW WenKai 1.530, Regular (400).

These reduced WOFF2 files are solely for /type-study/. They contain the fixed
preview text in sources.json plus printable ASCII. They must not be used as a
complete site Chinese font. The sources.json coverage counts describe the full
upstream originals BEFORE subsetting, not these small preview files.

Changes: subset glyph inventory; convert to WOFF2; rename internal family,
subfamily, full name, unique ID, and PostScript name to avoid Reserved Font Names.
Glyph outlines are not redrawn. Original copyright and licensing metadata remain.
The two unchanged OFL license files accompany these derivative webfont subsets.
Do not sell font software by itself. Keep the respective notices when distributing.
